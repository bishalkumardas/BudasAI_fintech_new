About BUDASAI

Built by investors. For investors.

BUDASAI exists because the Indian retail investor deserves the same quality of research, education and platform that institutions have had for decades.

Founder's Story

From the trading floor to your screen.

BUDASAI was founded after a decade of working with markets — across equities, credit and macro. We saw two recurring problems: investors had no access to honest, well-reasoned research, and the financial education available was either too shallow or too commercial.

So we built BUDASAI. A place to learn, to read deep research, and eventually to manage wealth — all under one principled roof.

Mission · Vision
