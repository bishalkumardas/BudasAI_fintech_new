Before You Invest

5 steps to take before investing

A simple, principled checklist every investor should run through.

1

Build an emergency fund

Keep 6 months of expenses in liquid, safe instruments before risking capital in markets.

2

Clear high-interest debt

Credit cards and personal loans almost always outpace market returns. Pay them off first.

3

Define your goal & horizon

Retirement, a home, education — each goal has a different time-frame and risk profile.

4

Learn the basics

Understand compounding, asset allocation, valuation and risk. Read before you click buy.

5

Start small, stay consistent

A small monthly SIP held for 20 years beats a large lump sum invested at the wrong time.
